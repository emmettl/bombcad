Rigid box mechanics

Gravity, friction and ground contact. Recorded from the Swift reference solver; no blast loading.

● Centre of mass · Side view (metres)

Side view along the y axis. All cases: mass 2 kg, static friction 0.6, sliding friction 0.5; impacts have no rebound. The model uses small positional corrections and needs timestep convergence checks. Cars, suspension, crushing and object-to-object collisions are not represented.
