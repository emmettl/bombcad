# Fragment data from Cooper's *Explosives Engineering*

`fragments.json` holds three things from P. W. Cooper, *Explosives Engineering*, Wiley-VCH, 1996,
chapter 27 (the user's scanned copy on `/Volumes/StudioData/bombcad/data/Cooper1996/`; book page =
PDF page − 16); only these derived numbers are kept, with citation.

- **Table 27.2** (p. 397), transcribed: Mott's coefficient B (lb^(1/2) in^(−7/16)) for mild
  steel cylinders, and the Chapman–Jouguet pressure (kbar), for thirteen explosives.
- **Figure 27.13** (p. 396): the experimental points of N(m), the number of fragments heavier
  than m (g), for a cast iron aerial bomb (from Cooper's ref. 16), fifteen points from 0.13 g to
  8.8 g. Found as solid dark blobs on the 300 dpi scan, mapped through the four fitted frame edges
  (the scan is turned 1.3°): about ±2% in each value. The counts are whole numbers in truth.
- **Figures 27.15–27.18** (pp. 400–402): Hoerner's drag coefficients against Mach number, 0.1–10,
  for a side-on cylinder, a sphere, a cube tumbling face-on and edge-on, an end-on cylinder and a
  face-on disc, sampled every 0.05 in log M. Traced on the 300 dpi scan along anchor points; the
  Mach axis fitted to the nineteen tick marks below it (within 3–6 px, about 2% in M), the drag
  axis to the frame's top and bottom edges, and the 1.3° turn of the scan taken out. About ±0.02
  in C_D. Checks: the sphere's 0.48 subsonic and 0.93 supersonic, and the cube's 1.03 face-on,
  are the usual values.
