# Nian et al. 2024: pressures before and behind an RC blast wall

X. Nian, Q. Xie, X. Kong, Y. Yao and K. Huang, "Experimental and numerical study on protective effect
of RC blast wall against air shock wave", *Defence Technology* 31 (2024) 567–579,
doi:10.1016/j.dt.2022.11.005, open access under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Only numbers derived from it are
kept here (no text or figures); the PDF stays off the repository (on StudioData,
`bombcad/data/Nian2024-BlastWall/`).

`nian2024.json` holds, each with its page, table or figure:

- the wall: reinforced concrete, 6.0 m long, 2.5 m high, 0.4 m thick, an inverted T on a buried
  footing, no wing walls (sandbags at its ends); bars and concrete strength not given;
- the shots: 5 and 20 kg of TNT on the ground on the wall's axis, 3 m from its front face; two shots,
  both with the wall (none without);
- the gauges: four side-on in the ground on a line through the charge parallel to the wall (3, 5,
  7, 9 m from it), three reflected in the wall's face (only the lowest, 0.22 m up, reported), three
  side-on in the ground on the axis 2, 4 and 6 m behind the wall (some invalid or filtered);
- the measured peaks: those in the text and tables, and readings of Fig. 15 (±0.005 MPa at 5 kg,
  ±0.02 MPa at 20 kg); the paper's empirical free-field reference behind the wall, with the 122.4
  kPa printed for 20 kg at 7.4 m corrected to the 167 kPa its formula gives;
- the paper's own AUTODYN model (2D axisymmetric) and its fitted protective-effect formula, for context.
