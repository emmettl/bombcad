# Bundled dry recordings

RoomCAD plays these recordings through a room to audition its response. All were recorded in, or
synthesized for, anechoic conditions. Each has been trimmed of leading silence and converted to FLAC;
the audio is otherwise unaltered. The licences below apply to these files only, not to RoomCAD's code.

## Operatic voice — `openair-operatic-voice.flac`

- Performer Marina Theodoropoulou; recording engineer Aglaia Foteinou.
- From the OpenAIR library (www.openairlib.net), University of York, anechoic recordings,
  "operatic-voice/mono/singing.wav" (48 kHz, 16-bit, mono), retrieved 7 October 2026 from
  https://webfiles.york.ac.uk/OPENAIR/Anechoic/operatic-voice/mono/singing.wav.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike, as stated on the item's OpenAIR page; OpenAIR's
  2025 collection page states Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 for the collection. This adapted file
  is shared under the same terms (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Changes: first 0.2 s removed; converted to FLAC.

## Violin pizzicato — `iowa-violin-pizzicato.flac`

- University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples, by Lawrence Fritts:
  violin, pizzicato, mezzo-forte, A string, A4 to B4 ("Violin.pizz.mf.sulA.A4B4.aiff", 44.1 kHz,
  16-bit, mono), recorded in an anechoic chamber with a Neumann KM 84, retrieved 7 October 2026 from
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin.html.
- Terms: "freely available on this website and may be downloaded and used for any projects, without
  restrictions" (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html).
- Changes: kept 1.95 s to 13.2 s of the original; converted to FLAC.
