Implementation plan — emmettl/bombcad-work#62, supporting emmettl/bombcad-work#63.

Predeclare a planar left-going compact bipolar acoustic pulse in ideal gas at 101325 Pa, 1.225 kg/m³, gamma 1.4. Domain x=0..16 m, rigid x-min and transverse faces, open x-max. Initial perturbation p'=-A sin(pi (x-8)) for |x-8|<1 m, otherwise zero; rho'=p'/c², u=-p'/(rho0 c), c=sqrt(gamma p0/rho0). A=1/10/100 Pa; nx=128/256/512, ny=nz=1; dx=16/nx. Stop at 14/c seconds, after direct and once-reflected pulses reach x≈4 m and the first cell next to the rigid wall. No afterburning, gravity, cooling, mapped start or numerical default change.

The independent linear-acoustic reference is p'(x,t)=f(x+c t-8)+f(c t-x-8), with matching opposite particle velocities. It is an exact linear solution, not a finite-amplitude Euler solution or blast/reflection experiment. The weak-amplitude assumption and Float32 initialization/projection error remain explicit. Direct first positive impulse=2 A/(pi c); near-wall first impulse=2 A(1+cos(pi x))/(pi c); near-wall peak=2 A cos(pi x). Pressure and closure timing errors are reported without fitting a gate.

Run all nine amplitude/resolution combinations with full-grid and tiled execution, each plain and consumed-pressure-observed (36 bounded numerical runs). Compare complete coarse state/recorder hashes for observation parity, retain every pressure history and initial pressure projection. Report both offline half-final-peak and streaming results, analytic pulse errors, negative-sample/closure state, tiled/full differences, and resolution trends. Do not infer convergence from monotonicity alone or hide failures. No policy/tolerance/mask/index/admission change. CPU analytical reference tests and existing pressure replay validate the harness. GPU work uses the cooperative slot and each invocation stays below 1.5 hours. This stage tests the ideal-gas numerical core, not the recommended hot-air/terrain product regime or CUDA parity; do not generalize it to those regimes.
