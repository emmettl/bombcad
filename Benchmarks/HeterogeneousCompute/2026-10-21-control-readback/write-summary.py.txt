## API trace: less explicit waiting is not necessarily less waiting

A separate, perturbed 568-step blast64 diagnostic uses the installed PyTorch profiler. It is
excluded from the timing table. Both traces launch 569 prepares, 1,707 sweeps and 570 first-phase
kernels (including initial recording and the terminal control iteration). Driver launches are
unchanged. Device-wide synchronization calls fall from 571 to 2, but blocking control readback
and stream waits remain: 1,188 `cudaStreamSynchronize` calls, 1,191 `cudaMemcpyAsync` calls, and
580 device-to-pageable-host transfers in each trace, including setup/output transfers.

The profiler attributes about 90.8 ms to baseline device-wide waits versus 0.028 ms in the
candidate; memcpy API time grows from about 30.4 to 89.3 ms. These are perturbed trace
observations, not additive performance predictions. They show waiting moving into the blocking
copy. Kernel timings and context warmup are also perturbed; only the uninstrumented repeated
worker measurements support the performance conclusion.
