**Draft, not released.** Notes for 0.6.0, gathered from main after 0.5.0's cut (the precast seats
on neoprene pads, `68c2327a`). The version, build and commit are filled in when the build is made;
the user decides when.

**New, as options.**

- **Structures' outlines as cut cells** (`structureCutCells`, off): a cell a structure's elements
  cross holds the air it has, its faces open as far as they are, as the terrain's cut cells do,
  with the ground, blocks and terrain cut cells taken as what they are
  ([structural model](../structural-model.md)). A free wall off the lattice takes its impulse
  within 2.6% of the line between its lattice values, where the staircase steps 6%, but its peak
  force overshoots 12% a tenth of a cell off it; 1.23 times the air's cost a cell. Merging small
  cells made the load 15% low and was dropped. Off until the peak is right.

**The record.**

- **Beirut against three more sources** ([Beirut](../beirut-2020.md)): the shock arrives at
  0.94–1.06 of Stennett et al.'s six observers' times; the grain silos, as a block, shade the
  peaks as Denny et al.'s silo tests measured at 2–3 m/kg^(1/3) and recover more slowly than their
  Viper::Blast farther out; the JRC's simulation (Valsamos et al., 2021) matches Kingery–Bulmash
  with a charge tuned to it, the figures quoted from it before being its Kingery–Bulmash values.

- **Beirut refined:** after the two refined-air fixes and the 500 Pa windows, the district on 8 m
  cells refined twice (about 35 minutes on the mini, 14 GB) loads its façades as 3 m cells do,
  median peaks 29, 17, 11 and 8 kPa at 0.5–1.5 km against 30, 18, 12 and 9, its peaks 0.80–0.86 of
  Kingery–Bulmash. But a ringing of about 0.5–0.9 kPa, carried by the refinement at some 2 km/s,
  runs ahead of the on-time shock south-east of the charge: gauges there take it for the arrival
  and some façades' windows open too early, leaving their panes unbroken. Its arrivals and damage
  are indicative until that is found.
- **The slab, wall and Keys comparisons off the balloon start:** Wu, Wang and Yao's slabs,
  Chiquito's, Alsubaei's walls, Keys's walls in the open, Mendonça's studies on today's air and the
  Kasun cubicle now start a lone charge from the one-dimensional solution, as the app does, at the
  open faces' clearance. Against the balloon in the same domains: Chiquito's P7 left 111 → 121 mm,
  Keys's debris 1.60 → 1.75 m out, Wang's P1 peak 10% lower, Yao's T4 underside damage 29% more,
  the rest within 6%; no standing moved. The chamber followed with the mapped start for every
  charge (below).
- **Keys's debris judged by its side:** in the open it lies too far out, the safe side for its
  reach; in the shock tube it is thrown half as far as measured, the unsafe side; and the walls
  fall as one column where the tests' folded, so how they come apart is wrong either way.

- **Footings on a slope** ([structural model](../structural-model.md#footings),
  [validation](../validation.md#footings-on-a-slope)): footings stay level, stepped into the
  slope, and read the ground round them from the terrain: each side buried as deep as the surface
  stands over the base, Coulomb's earth pressures for ground rising or falling away, and the
  bearing capacity cut by Vesić's (1 − tan β)², 0.88–1.39 of Meyerhof's charts on a slope's face
  and 0.85–1.14 near a 20° crest. Judged on closed forms only. A new sample, "Two-storey frame on
  a hillside".

**Defaults changed.**

- **Every charge starts from a finely resolved burst,** not only a lone one: each is solved in one
  dimension and mapped to its own radius (0.8 of the way to its nearest solid, pane or face, half
  way to the nearest other charge), and a charge on a reflecting face, a model's mirror plane or
  corner, is mirrored as one on the ground (2, 4 or 8 times its mass). Two 10 kg bursts 30 m apart:
  peaks within the mapped radius 98–115% of Kingery–Bulmash where the balloon gave 52–69%, impulses
  within 8% of the curves superposed. The chamber's four charges now take it too, moving its
  results 3% or less. A lone charge in the open is as before, bit for bit.
- **Structures on the terrain:** the clamp and the ground's connection also hold a base within
  half an element of the terrain under it, not only at z = 0, and a structure's loose nodes,
  shell nodes and rubble land on the terrain instead of the floor. Scenes without terrain are
  unchanged.

- **An envelope's load history from the blast's arrival starts at 500 Pa at the component,** not
  when the air around it wakes, which is a tile ahead of the shock: Beirut's windows had opened
  0.13–0.35 s before their load, and on refined air most of a half-second window closed before it
  came. Options saved before keep the old rule.

**Fixed.**

- **A structure standing on cut terrain went non-finite as the blast arrived:** cut cells less
  than half air under it were shut in and their columns' differences came back to them magnified.
  They are now solid for the run, as under a block. Refined runs with such a structure keep the
  terrain as a staircase until the same rule reaches the refined levels.
- **A structure on footings never settled under its own weight** and started the blast unloaded:
  its footing joints chattered at rest, a few cm/s at microns, as the joint was worked out from
  the scene's positions and its stiffness turned their rounding into a fresh traction every step.
  It is now worked out from displacements, and the frame on a hillside settles in 0.18 s. Every
  footing moves a little: FoRDy's shaken events by up to 2.4 mrad in peak rotation, FoRCy's slow
  test by under 0.004 in its moment ratios. A body still moving at the 1 s limit whose
  displacement has stood still over its last 5 ms also counts as settled now; bodies that settled
  before are unchanged.

- **Refined air beside a building lost pressure when its patches were released.** A coarse cell
  cut by a block's finer outline averaged its fine cells' air over its own share, not the air's,
  and came out short by the block's share (half its fine air's pressure in the test); released
  patches left that deficit behind. Terrain-only scenes are unchanged. Suspected of Beirut's
  negative precursor at 1 km, which is being rerun.

## To consider before 0.6.0

Raised in 0.5.0's review, left for after its build:

- **Collapse that has not been judged reads as the concrete band.** The large-panel street
  preset's damage stands as an *approximation*, as any concrete structure's does, though the one
  blind test of collapse (Mendonça, blind test 2) missed it. Whether the standing should withhold
  or downgrade collapse-type results (progressive collapse, a storey lost, a structure down) for
  structures whose collapse has not been compared, rather than letting them inherit the band of the
  material's local damage.
