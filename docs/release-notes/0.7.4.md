Fourteenth build of BombCAD, an interactive blast simulator for Macs.

- macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon (arm64), Metal GPU
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, with the ticket stapled: it opens without
  Gatekeeper warnings
- Version 0.7.4, build 19, from commit 56cc7a8e

**Install with Homebrew:** `brew install emmettl/tap/bombcad` installs the app and links its
headless runner as `bombcad`.

**The main changes**, each in full below:
- **The quick estimate no longer depends on how the city lies on the grid.** Façades now face as
  their buildings' map outlines, not the solver's staircase of cells: with Beirut turned 45°, the
  same façades read within 1.5% of each other, where before they moved by up to 12%. Districts laid
  out before 28 October keep the old facing until made again with File ▸ New District.
- **Far façades past 2 km read nearer the runs**, on terms fitted to districts reaching 3 km.
- **Stone walls' damage chances are calibrated**, every tenth within 0.06 of the runs.
- **The drag preview follows the full estimate again** with the new facing.
- **Corrupt GeoTIFF and IFC files are refused with a message** instead of ending the app.
- **Structures on their own, and every structure settling before a blast, run 7–8% faster.**
- **A large-panel block's collapse is quoted as a range** between two ways of taking its hinges'
  energy.
- Every earlier setting stays selectable by name, to reproduce earlier estimates.
- **Worker protocol unchanged (19):** Macs on 0.7.2, 0.7.3 and 0.7.4 can share work.

## New

**A new validation case: a steel column's web breached close in** (for developers). `blastbench
dinu` meshes a HEB260 column as shells that tear, under 1.8 kg of TNT at 0.2 and 0.5 m from its web
(the CODEC bunker tests reported by FRAMEBLAST), and under the full-scale building's 2.2 and 2.5 kg
at 0.2 m. On 10 and 5 mm shells the web holds at 0.5 m and is breached at 0.2 m, as tested; on
20 mm shells it holds. The breach's length is not judged here: these notes first compared it with
0.3 m, a misreading, where the tests' own paper gives almost 500 and 600 mm, and the case is set up
and judged to that paper after this release. The full-flanged column is holed by 2.2 kg where the
building's only dished. Nothing changes in runs; details in
[a column web breached close in](../validation.md#a-column-web-breached-close-in).

**A candidate for the glass chances: pulses that arrive with a front.** A run's façade load
arrives as a shock, a share of its peak, and a pane overshoots under it; the surrogate's triangle
rising from nothing does not, and broke too little glass far out. With Envelope Damage ▸ "Glass
pulses with a front (candidate)" (headless `--surrogate-terms clear-top-front`, benches
`--terms clear-top-front`), the glass chances' pulses arrive with a share of their peak at once,
fitted on the surrogate's own fit runs: none within Z 8, about a fifth at Z 24, about half from Z 49.
Against Beirut's clear-top runs, some glass broken scores Brier 0.006 against 0.028, thrown past
3 m 0.017 against 0.047, and blown out still passes; the far field's error at 0.2 kt halves (some
broken at 1.75–2 km 0.75 against 0.46, the run 0.90). It is off by default: some broken and thrown
still fail the protective-design test, on the far loads beyond 2 km, and charge inference from
glass alone reads lower (0.023 kt against 0.055). Details are in
[glass by chance](../glass-probability.md#why-some-broken-and-thrown-past-3b-read-low-the-pulses-front-and-the-far-loads-25-october-2026).

## Changed

**Façades deep in a city: far terms past 2 km are the default.** The surrogate's terms were fitted
on runs reaching 1.56 km, and read Beirut's clear-top runs 14–21% low at 2.25–2.75 km. New fit runs
reach 3 km across two made-up dense districts. Far terms fitted on them raise the façade peaks
behind more than 30 buildings and on faces turned away beyond 1.75 km, and leave every nearer load
as it was. Against Beirut's clear-top runs the far peaks read 0.87–0.98 of the run's at 2.25–3 km,
against 0.78–0.90, and the glass chances' some broken scores Brier 0.019 against 0.028. Adopted on
25 October 2026, they are now the default for the quick estimate and its drag preview, the glass
and walls' chances, the far field, the close calls and the protection planner. Nearer the charge
nothing moves: the walls' chances are the same bit for bit. The terms of earlier that day stay by
name: Envelope Damage ▸ "Façade terms of 25 October (earlier estimates)", headless
`--surrogate-terms clear-top`, benches `--terms clear-top`. The glass front refitted alongside stays
an option, off by default ("Glass pulses with a front", `deep-city-front`): with it, blown out still
over-reads near 1.5–2 km. Charge inference keeps the terms of 23 October. Details are in
[the surrogate](../surrogate-first-look.md#adopted-the-deep-city-terms-25-october-2026).

**The judges can split their figures by the line's angle to the solver's cells.** `surrogate-judge`,
`hybrid-judge` and `glass-calibrate` take `--by-angle`, which also gives each figure on the lines
diagonal to the cells alone and balanced by angle. For a few days the standings quoted the diagonal
figure beside each adopted one, on the view that the grid takes least off those lines. With Beirut
turned 45° to the cells, the runs' loads went with the façades whatever their angle, so the diagonal
lines are not the cleaner ones; the split by angle was the quick estimate's own, its facing read off
the cells' staircase faces. The standings no longer quote it. No default changes. Details are in
[the surrogate](../surrogate-first-look.md#façade-incidence-from-map-outlines-28-october-2026).

**Façades face as their buildings' map outlines, by default.** The quick estimate and its drag
preview, the glass chances, the far field and the close calls now take each wall's facing from its
building's map outline, not from the solver's cells' staircase of faces, on the deep-city terms
refitted so. The estimate no longer depends on how the city lies on the grid: with Beirut turned
45° to the cells, the same façades read within 1.5% of each other, where the cells' facing moved
them by −12% to +8%. On Beirut as it lies every judge keeps its verdict and moves by 0.01–0.02
(glass by side 68–89% as the run's; far-field peaks 0.87–1.09 of the run's). Districts laid out
before 28 October 2026 carry no outlines and keep the cells' facing: make them again with File ▸ New
District to take it. The protection planner stays on the deep-city terms, charge inference on those
of 23 October, and the glass front stays an option. The terms before stay by name: Envelope Damage
▸ "Façades facing as the cells (earlier estimates)", headless `--surrogate-terms deep-city`,
benches `--terms deep-city`. Details are in
[the surrogate](../surrogate-first-look.md#adopted-facing-as-the-outlines-28-october-2026).

**Stone walls' chances are calibrated.** The quick estimate's chance that a building's masonry
walls are damaged now takes the stone walls' load ×0.85 with the whole load scatter, where it took
×1.0 with half of it. Against Beirut's ten near runs refined to 2 m, each building taken as 0.5 m
stone, every tenth of chance now comes within 0.06 of the runs' share (Brier skill 0.75), where
before the middle chances read up to 0.31 high. Concrete walls, roofs, glass and the estimate's
damage levels don't change, nor does anything on Beirut's map, whose buildings are concrete.
Details are in
[the walls' chances](../glass-probability.md#adopted-stone-085-with-the-whole-scatter-29-october-2026).

**Structures alone, and every structure settling under its own weight, run 7–8% faster.** In steps
without air or a prescribed load on their faces, the solid elements' pass runs in two: a lighter
kernel for the concrete that has not cracked, crushed or yielded, and the full one for the rest.
A 225,000-element building alone runs 1,481 steps a second against 1,367, with contact 1,258
against 1,175, and settles before a coupled run in 13.3 s against 14.3 s; coupled steps are
unchanged. Results move at the rounding level, as on another GPU: within the spread the
`--rounding` builds already show, no judged outcome changed
([performance](../performance.md#structure-step-cost)).

## Fixed

**A corrupt GeoTIFF or IFC is refused with a message instead of ending BombCAD.** A BigTIFF
value past 2⁶³ in a strip's offset or count, no samples per pixel, or sizes whose product overflows ended
the app; a width of 2³² − 1 ran it out of memory, and thousands of samples a pixel held it for
minutes. A strip or tile cut short, which read its missing rows as elevations of 0, and an
infinite sample, cell size or tie point, which placed the terrain at infinity, are refused too.
An IFC whose geometry lies impossibly far off (past ten million kilometres) is refused, naming
the line, where it ended the app too. Valid files read as before. Found by fuzzing the GeoTIFF
reader and the IFC importer on a Mac
([Linux](../linux.md#fuzzing-on-a-mac-and-threadsanitizer-on-the-slow-suites-28-october-2026));
each case is a test in ElevationFileRobustnessTests or IFCGeometryRobustnessTests.

**The drag preview's glass follows the full estimate again with façades facing as their outlines.**
Since the outline facing became the default, a building side whose outline turns (a cut corner, or
two slanted fronts) let its one nearest strip, facing the charge squarely, stand for strips facing
away, so the preview showed glass blown out that the full estimate leaves whole. Each side's glass
now takes the nearest strip of each way its outline faces it. On Beirut at 0.5 kt the preview's
blown-out glass is within 0.1 of the full estimate's on 86% of sides again (82% before the fix),
its bias +0.013 (+0.024); the damage and the walls' chances are unchanged. Details are in
[the surrogate](../surrogate-first-look.md#the-drag-preview-facing-as-the-outlines-29-october-2026).

## Known limitations

**A large-panel block's collapse is quoted as a range.** The collapse's standing (still
illustrative, with no band) now carries the spread from how its hinges' energy is taken: today's
element, where hourglass control takes most of it, against the one that bends in its cracked
concrete (assumed strain, five times the cost). Under the street charge twelve storeys lose
245–321 m² of floor, every storey, with 21,000–54,000 elements failed and 31–80 t of rubble; three
storeys lose 15–79% of their floor. Assumed strain is the more damaging end and errs that way on
the member tests; neither end is a bound, and the debris's reach, still creeping on the hard ground
when the runs end, is not quoted. For developers, `blastbench wholeevent --outcome` reads these
from a run's checkpoints. See
[a range](../whole-event.md#a-range-28-october-2026).
