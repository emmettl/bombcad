**People in the scene, illustrative:** once a quick estimate has the glass's chances, the
sidebar's People in the scene section places people in each building by its use and the time of
day, and gives the people it would expect hurt, with a band: by glass, the walls and roofs, and
the blast itself. Colour Buildings by People Hurt colours each building by its share hurt, and
follows a drag. It rests on published models from other events and hazards: Hazus's people by
time of day and its earthquake casualty rates, Kummer's glass injury, and Eisenberg's probits. On
Beirut 2020 its counts read 4 to 12 times the injuries and deaths recorded, where it finds the
hurt agrees better, and the section says so first: compare places of the charge and protections
with it, not counts ([people in the scene](../../people-in-the-scene.md)).
