**People in the scene, illustrative and relative:** once a quick estimate has the glass's
chances, the sidebar's People in the scene section places people in each building by its use and
the time of day, and shows where they would be hurt as shares, by building and by distance. Pin a
baseline, then move the charge, change the time of day or protect a building, and it gives the
change, such as "−38% hurt against the pinned baseline", draw by draw over the same assumptions.
Colour Buildings by People Hurt colours each building by its share hurt, and follows a drag. It
rests on published models from other events and hazards: Hazus's people by time of day and its
earthquake casualty rates, Kummer's glass injury, and Eisenberg's probits. The counts and their
band are behind Show counts, under the warning that on Beirut 2020 they read 4 to 12 times the
injuries and deaths recorded; where it puts the hurt agrees better
([people in the scene](../../people-in-the-scene.md)).
