**Gauges read at the wall have a standing of their own.** With `gaugesReadWallPressure` on, the
standing lists "Gauges read at the wall" as an approximation for peaks and impulses, and a gauge it
reads at a wall carries a note instead of the reflected bands, which were derived from the cell
beside the wall: the read brings peaks nearer Kingery–Bulmash but overshoots the close-in impulse
([air-blast model](../../air-blast-model.md#reflected-peaks-at-a-wall)). Off, nothing changes.
