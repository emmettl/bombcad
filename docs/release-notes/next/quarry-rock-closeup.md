**In the Quarry Face window and its clips, rock that has not been fired reads as whole rock, and the broken rock close to the camera reads as stones rather than paper-thin shards.** Before a firing, the face and the ridge's crest no longer show every block's outline as a crack: the blocks' outer faces lie on one weathered surface, banded by its beds, with a few joints, and each block takes its own colour and roughness only once it moves. The small chips are chunkier, no larger than about an eighth of the block they come from, and only leave blocks that are open to the air, so a ridge's crest is no longer strewn with thin flakes as it breaks. The weathered faces' bump is gentler, which took away the dark blue spots on rock facing the sky. Illustrative, for the look only: the rock, its beds and its breakage are stylised, and nothing here is a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md), [docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md)).
