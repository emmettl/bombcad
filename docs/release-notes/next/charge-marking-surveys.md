**Read the Charge from the Damage now takes a survey, keeps its marks with the project, reaches the far field, and is no longer far too sure with many marks.** **Load a Survey…** reads a marks CSV, GEER's façade shapefile, its structural tables, or a folder of them. It puts them on the scene's buildings and says how many matched and how many did not. On Beirut, GEER's survey marked 1,354 buildings, and its 50 stone buildings read 0.70 kt (90%: 0.21–2.16 kt) with Warehouse 12 inside the place's regions. The marks are saved with the project (older projects open as before), and New District's far buildings can be marked too. The marks are now weighed with an error they all share, a factor 1.65 in yield and 12 m in place, because neighbours judged by the same estimate err together. On a made-up district held out, at 30 marks the yield's 90% interval held the true yield 98–99% of the time, against 84–85% before, and with every building marked 100% against 19%, the median errors no worse. Past 100 marks the panel warns that the answer is likely still too sure: with every building marked the place's 90% region held the truth 70% of the time there, and on Beirut only 28%. Stage 2's weighing stays selectable as "independent". Standing: illustrative ([charge inference](../../charge-inference.md#marks-that-err-together-surveys-and-kept-marks-2-november-2026)).
