**The learned field model's weights download when you first turn it on, and only when you click
Download.** The app shows the file's size (15.7 MB), where it comes from (the repository's
`field-model-1` release) and what it is for. It checks the file against the SHA-256 the app
expects before keeping it in Application Support. A failed or cancelled download keeps nothing,
and the option stays off, saying why. The release's notes give the model's training data, its
licences and its illustrative standing
([how the weights ship](../../learned-models.md#how-the-weights-ship-21-october-2026)).
