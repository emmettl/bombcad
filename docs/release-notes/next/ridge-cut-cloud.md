**In the Ridge cut window and its clips, the dust cloud now reads as pale sunlit dust, its cracks' jets as thin spurts, and Record Clip's Pull back opens on the whole crest swelling.** The cloud from the firings no longer has near-black cores: sunlight now diffuses through thick dust instead of dying out in it, so the shaded side of a column is a darker tan and its sunward rims bright, and the speckle of dark rock through thin dust over the heap is gone, so the rock running down the near flank shows through. The cracks in the rising dome jet thin, fanned spurts of grit instead of round puffs. Pull back now opens a little wider with sky over the crest, so the whole dome is seen rising, and moves out from the first firing as the rock breaks. The rock's motion is unchanged, the window's draw is about 0.3 ms more while the ridge fires, and the Quarry Face is unchanged. Illustrative, like the rest of the window: dust for the eye, not a model of real dust or a blast-design aid ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3i").
