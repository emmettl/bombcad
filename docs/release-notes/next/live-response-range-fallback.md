**The drag no longer colours buildings from inputs its networks have never seen:** where a building
lies outside what the learned response was trained on, its preview while dragging is the SDOF's and
draws, as for a construction the response cannot read, and the drag says how many and why, as the
user decided on 3 November 2026. The training's ranges now ship with the weights, so the drag and
the check of the built-in scenes read the same ones. The presets on medium cells (the office tower,
the transport hub and the process plant) are now previewed by the SDOF throughout; on the explorer's
square and street a few buildings nearest a large charge are (under 1%), at no cost to the frame
rate. The explorer's Beirut stays on the learned response for now, with a note: on the SDOF its walls'
drag would fall to a few frames a second
([learned models](../../learned-models.md#what-lies-outside-the-training-on-the-cpu-3-november-2026)).
