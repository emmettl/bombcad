**Pitched roofs.** Shells can now be laid at a pitch, so a roof can have real slopes, a ridge and gables. The structure editor's *Add Pitched Roof* sets the pitch, the ridge's direction and the covering. It puts two raftered slopes over the structure from the tops of its walls, raises the end walls to gables, seats the slopes on the walls under them and hangs the tiles on their battens. On import, *Roof slabs at a pitch as shells at their pitch* brings an IFC roof slab in at its pitch; without it, such a slab stays a rigid obstacle. The terraced row now has a 40° roof with purlins, and its tiles hang on their nibs instead of sliding off. The seats' and nibs' laws are assumed from how roofs are built, not measured. No pitched roof under blast was found to compare with, so the standing table lists *Shells at a pitch* as an approximation (docs/shell-model.md#shells-at-a-pitch).
