**A ridge cut now opens close on its crest, so the crest can be seen breaking and the notch forming, and Record Clip has a Pull back camera.** The Quarry Face window's ridge cut is first seen from about 95 m (it was about 190 m), off the cut's steeper flank and a little under the crest's height, so the crest and the notch stand against the sky with the near flank the rock comes down below them, and the cut's wall facing you is in the sun. The view is worked out from the cut itself, so a moved, resized or reversed cut, or one on a terrain file's or the project's ground, is framed the same way; it is still an ordinary view you can orbit. *Pull back* (Record Clip ▸ Camera) starts close in on the crest, holds until just after the first firing, then eases back to your view over five seconds of the event as the rock spills, at any clip speed.
