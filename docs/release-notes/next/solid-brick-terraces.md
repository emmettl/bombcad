**A "Terraced houses, pre-1919 solid brick" sample:** the terraced row with solid one-brick
walls, as most British houses standing in 1940 had, beside the 1930s cavity-walled one. Under
the wartime bombs' 96 kg its houses grade within a category of the Ministry of Home Security's
surveys at every distance by 0.3 s, their fronts standing by arching between the party walls,
where the cavity row's front leaves go over, one or two categories too severe beyond 11 m. The
standing of collapse is unchanged
