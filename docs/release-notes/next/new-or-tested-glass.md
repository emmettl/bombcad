**New or tested glass, a construction's option.** A building's glass can be judged as designed
(weathered, E1300's strength, still the default) or as new or tested glass, 1.55 times as strong:
the least of Osnes et al.'s tested panes. Set it under **Glass** in the construction editor. It
applies to envelope damage, the façade glass chances and protective design, where re-glazing with
new glass is also a change a plan may make, at your cost. Its standing notes that new glass
weathers in service, and that at a little more strength (1.58) one of Osnes's cracked panes would
hold ([glass strength](../../glass-strength.md#the-option-new-or-tested-glass)).
