**In the Quarry Face window, the event now plays as it is simulated: Fire again starts it in about a tenth of a second, and a strip dragged to a new place starts in about 3 s.** Before, a refire took 16–24 s, and a moved strip another 5–10 s, before anything moved. The solver writes each frame to the stored event as it goes, and playback follows it. It plays at real time when the solver keeps ahead, and at the busiest part of the event slows to about half speed rather than skipping frames; the readout says so while it does. The timeline scrubs over what has been simulated so far, and over the whole 20 s once it is done. The old event stays on view until the new one can start. What you watch is the stored run itself, not a second simulation. Illustrative, like the rest of the window ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md), "Fire and watch at once").
