**Steel members buckling under a sudden end load, checked against tests.** Kenny's drop-hammer
tests on steel and aluminium strips (Dalhousie, 2001) are now a benchmark (`blastbench kenny`).
The final deflection of the peak buckle comes within half to twice of measured in 13 of 16
series with the default Eurocode bows, and in 15 of 16 with Kenny's measured imperfections. The
slenderest steel strips read 2.5–3.7 times too far with the default bows. The model's buckle
lies near mid-length, where the tests' lay near the clamp. The steel needs its strain-rate law:
without it the strips read 1.3–7 times too far ([buckling](../../shell-model.md#buckling)).
