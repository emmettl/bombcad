**A district in minutes: the air near the charge, the surrogate beyond (File ▸ New District, and
headless as `scenebench district-hybrid`).** The air runs in 3-D only out to a hand-over radius.
Beyond it, each façade's load comes from the façade load surrogate, its peak and impulse matched to
the near run where both hold and carried on the near run's trend with distance. On Beirut, handing
over at 1 km, the near run took 135–335 s on the CI mini against the full run's 1,604–2,464 s, and
laying out and judging all 14,169 buildings about a minute more on the Studio. Inside the hand-over
the near run's loads are the full run's. Beyond it, sides with some glass broken come within 0.03
of the full run's to 1.5 km at every yield (to 2 km at 0.5 kt, 2.5 km at 1.1 kt), and arrivals come
2–4% earlier. It holds less at 0.2 kt beyond 1.5 km (0.13–0.25 under) and for blown-out glass in
some bands, and walls and roofs beyond the hand-over keep the surrogate's standing. In the app, New
District hands over at 12 m/kg^(1/3) when the radius reaches 1.25 times that. The buildings beyond
are kept beside the project, never voxelised, and saved with it. When the run ends they are judged
in seconds and painted with their damage, labelled a far field estimate with its match and
standing. The app carries the trend to 1.5 times the hand-over and holds it from there; at 0.2 kt
that brought the share broken at 1.75–2 km to 0.43 against the full run's 0.61, from 0.36 ([a city
in minutes](../../hybrid-far-field.md)).
