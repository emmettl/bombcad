**Security film and toughened glass as envelope constructions.** A building's panes can carry
security film, 4–14 mil, daylight, wet glazed or mechanically attached, and its glass can be
toughened, set in the construction editor. Once the glass cracks, the film holds the fragments as
a membrane until its anchorage lets go. Against 67 open GSA tests of filmed 1/4" panes:
- wet-glazed panes stay, condition 2, as 18 of 24 were graded, the model never worse;
- daylight film's sheets stay while pushed and go on the rebound, one class lighter than most tests;
- mechanical attachments, judged on a vendor's capacity, pull out, never better than a test.
