**Drag the charge and the quick estimate follows it.** With a quick estimate on screen and Place
Charge on, drag the charge across the ground. While you drag, a preview colours the buildings by
what the view shows: the damage, the glass's chances or the walls' and roofs'. It judges each
building side by its component nearest the charge, and the sidebar marks it as a preview. When you
let go, the full estimate is made where the charge stopped. On Beirut's 14,000 buildings a preview
frame takes 1–2 s on a busy Studio, about a second for the damage when it is less busy. Against the
full estimate, its glass chances were within 0.1 on 88–93% of sides and its walls' and roofs' on
98.9–100% of buildings, and the damage's colour was the same for 99.7%. The quick estimate itself now
lays the district out once, which also saves working out the glass's sight lines twice
([dragging the charge](../../surrogate-first-look.md#dragging-the-charge-20-october-2026)).
