**In a ridge cut, each firing now throws up its own column of dust, the rock running down the flank trails a skirt of dust, and the notch shows again about two seconds after the last firing.** Before, the dust rose from the crest as one smooth cloud that arched over the cut and hung there for the rest of the event, hiding the notch, with little dust where the rock came down. Each firing's dust is now a narrow plume from the ground above it, so the order of the firings reads along the cut; the plumes thin as they rise, and the ridge's dust clears in a second or two once nothing feeds it. Blocks sliding and rolling on the flanks below the cut's floor kick up dust that goes on down the slope with them, and blows anywhere on the ridge throw up more dust than before. All of it still comes from the firings and the rock: no dust is added that does not follow the rock. The same in the window and in clips; the dust costs the same to run, and the Quarry Face's dust is unchanged. Illustrative, like the rest of the window ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 2a").
