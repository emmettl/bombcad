**Read the charge back from marked damage, in milliseconds (headless for now).** `blastmodel charge-from-damage infer` (also `scenebench`) takes buildings marked glass whole or broken and walls undamaged, damaged or heavily damaged, and gives the charge's likely place and yield with their spread in a few milliseconds; each further mark adds under half a millisecond. A small network learned from the quick estimate's own chances over Beirut and four made-up districts gives it, trained on the PC. On charges drawn from those chances its 50% and 90% regions hold the truth as often as they claim, it comes within about 10% of the full posterior's yield, and it does as well on a district it never saw. On Beirut's surveys it inherits the quick estimate's biases: GEER's glass reads tens of tonnes, not the 0.3–1.1 kt the arrivals give. Standing: illustrative ([charge inference](../../charge-inference.md#the-charge-from-marked-damage-learned-1-november-2026)). The network is built in, so the commands need no weights file; the app has no panel for it yet.
