**VoiceOver and the keyboard reach the view, its legends and chart, and name every control.** The
3D view reads as "3D view" with a summary of the scene (its size, the charge, what is in it, the
time and what the surfaces show), and VoiceOver's actions orbit and zoom it; **View ▸ Camera** does
the same from the keyboard, ⌥⌘ and an arrow to orbit in 15° steps, ⌘= and ⌘− to zoom. The colour
scales read as their range, the chart below the view by what it plots and as VoiceOver's audio graph,
and each gauge's peak with its name. Every slider now reads its title and the value shown ("TNT
equivalent, 50 kg"), not its bare number; fields that read their name twice read it once; each
kept run's buttons in Compare Runs say which run; and each building in Envelope building damage has
**Select This Building**, so its chances and its fire switch are reached without clicking the view.
