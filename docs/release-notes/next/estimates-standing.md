**The quick estimate, the hybrid far field and charge inference take their standing from the
standing table**, as runs do: their figures against Beirut's full runs and BombCAD's own charges
are in [validation.md](../../validation.md#estimates-beside-a-run), and the lines each prints are
worded from them and count in the table's identity. The quick estimate's far loads now read 1.1–1.6
times the run's on the median, as its page has it, not "up to ×1.6–2". `scenebench infer-charge`
prints its standing and writes it into `--out`'s JSON.
