**The cheapest glazing and stand-off changes that meet a glass hazard target, headless.**
`scenebench protect` takes a district, a threat (a charge, weighted places or an inferred charge's
samples), a target per side (no pane thrown worse than a GSA condition except with an accepted
chance) and the user's costs for toughened, laminated or filled-in glazing and stand-off lines, and
ranks the plans that meet it, cheapest first, with each side's chance from the façade load
surrogate's draws. It finds the protective-design prototype's plans exactly on the prototype's own
problems, and confirms a plan on a run's saved façade histories where one exists. Plans are
illustrative until a run confirms them; walls, film and barriers are not yet searched
([protective design](../../protective-design.md#in-bombcad-scenebench-protect)).
