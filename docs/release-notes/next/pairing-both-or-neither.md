**Pairing two Macs leaves both paired or neither.** A sharing Mac whose user chose Allow after the
other Mac had stopped waiting (five minutes) kept the pairing and said it was paired, with a Mac
that had kept nothing; now it keeps the key only once the other Mac is known to be still there,
and that Mac only once the sharing Mac has kept it (emmettl/bombcad-work#76). See
