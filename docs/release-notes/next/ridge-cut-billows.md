**In the Ridge cut window and its clips, the firings' dust now billows instead of hanging in smooth cotton-wool balls, a thin veil of dust runs over the ground with the rock, and Record Clip's Pull back shows the whole crest.** The columns' puffs break into rounded lobes, bright toward the sun with darker creases between, and their edges tear and thin into haze; the lobes rise and roll with the dust and hold steady from frame to frame rather than crawling. Rock running over the ground now drags a low, thin, rolling sheet of dust along with it, so the cloud visibly follows the rock down. The first cracks no longer leave small soft puffs over the dome, and a near crack seen from above no longer shows as a pale smudge on the rock. Pull back's opening is a little wider and aimed a little to the right and lower, so the crest's far end is in the frame. The rock's motion is unchanged, the draw is up to about 1 ms more while the ridge fires, and the Quarry Face is unchanged. Illustrative, like the rest of the window: dust for the eye, not a model of real dust or a blast-design aid ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3j").
