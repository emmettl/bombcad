**In the Quarry Face window and its clips, rock that has not been fired reads as one strong mass with its own joints, not as a mosaic of blocks.** A continuous weathered skin now covers the unbroken rock, at the face, past both ends of the strip and on the ridge cut's crest, and each piece of it goes only when the block under it starts to move, so the blocks appear as the rock breaks. The weathered rock has joints that wander and die out, dark open cracks along them, knobs and hollows, small steps where laminae have weathered off its harder beds, pits, lichen and darker crusts; the rock round a ridge cut has the same. Illustrative, for the look only: the rock, its beds, joints and breakage are stylised, and nothing here is a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md), [docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md)).
