**Mark the damage on buildings and watch the charge firm up.** Simulation ▸ Read the Charge from the Damage… opens a panel beside the view: click a building to mark its glass broken or whole and its walls undamaged, damaged or heavily damaged, and the chance of where the charge was is painted on the ground with its likeliest half and nine tenths outlined, its yield drawn beside, and both said in words that VoiceOver reads. Each mark shows within a frame (under 1 ms to weigh, a 7 ms frame). Mark from the Damage Shown marks every building a run or the quick estimate judged, so the solver can referee: on a made-up district the network never saw, 30 marks put the charge about 90 m off and its yield within a factor 2 (median), every building 30 m and a factor 1.4, though then far too sure. Move the Charge There puts the scene's charge at the reading.
