**In the Quarry Face window you can now lay the strip on the face in the 3-D view, choose where a terrain file is cropped, break the open project's own terrain, and record a clip.** Turn on Place strip and drag along a face: the strip snaps to the face line under the cursor, is drawn over the view with the line of the new face behind it, and its ends can be dragged; on release the rock is broken as from the map, which stays. Opening a terrain file shows an overview of the whole file to drag the area (300 m to 1.2 km) to; a project with terrain open in BombCAD is offered too, and either starts with a strip along its steepest face. Record Clip… writes an .mp4 at 1920 × 1080 from the current view or a slow orbit, of a chosen stretch of the event at real time, half or quarter speed (slow motion draws the moments between stored frames), with progress and Cancel while the window keeps playing. Opening a terrain file whose corner is far from (0, 0), which most surveys are, no longer fails. Illustrative, like the rest of the window: stylised firing patterns, not a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md), "Placing the strip, choosing the ground, and clips").
