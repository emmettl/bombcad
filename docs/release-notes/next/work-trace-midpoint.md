**The structure work trace no longer counts work that was not done** (for developers). The
`--work` trace counted each step's work at the stress after it, which over-counts whenever a
structure rings. It now uses the mean of the stresses before and after each step. Whole-event
runs' energy budgets now close: with this and the rubble fixes, the twelve-storey block's net external work by 3.36 s
went from 52 MJ to −4.6 MJ. Mechanism shares quoted from the trace moved by up to a fifth on ringing
slabs and by under 1.5% on members loaded once. Runs themselves are unchanged.
