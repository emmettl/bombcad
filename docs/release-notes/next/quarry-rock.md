**The Quarry Face's rock has beds of different kinds, weathered and fresh faces, and gravel on the pile.** Each bed of the face is pale limestone, dark shale, buff sandstone or red-brown ironstone, so the face is banded in colour and in relief (shale weathers back under ledges), and each kind breaks its own way: limestone into large blocks, shale into slabs and flakes, sandstone into elongated pieces, with some corners knocked off. Rock within a few metres of the old surface is weathered and breaks buff and brown; deeper rock breaks pale and grey, so the pile is a mix rather than one colour. Gravel and grit fill the hollows between the blocks at rest and spread in an apron at the pile's foot, and dust thrown up low over the pile falls out within a couple of seconds instead of lying in a flat sheet. Illustrative, for the look only: the beds and their kinds are stylised, not any real quarry's geology, and nothing here is a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md)).
