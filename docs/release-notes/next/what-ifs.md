**Instant what-ifs:** with a quick estimate on screen, holding the charge's size slider re-colours the
city live, about 25 ms a frame on Beirut on a Mac Studio, and the full estimate follows when it is let
go. A new **What if every building** group under Quick Estimate sets every building's glass
(toughened, filmed, laminated), walls (concrete, blockwork, masonry, sheeting, timber) and roof,
re-colouring at once, then making the full estimate; the yield's spread does the same. A what-if is
for the estimate only and never changes the project. It rests on the live drag's learned response,
now trained over the envelope model's constructions and glazing on Beirut and four made-up
districts, so that it no longer falls back for districts built otherwise. Against the full estimate
it is as close as the CPU's preview on Beirut and on each control moved, and closer on the glass; on
a made-up district it never saw, the damage colour matched on 98.3–98.4% of buildings against the
CPU preview's 99.6–99.7%
([instant what-ifs](../../learned-models.md#instant-what-ifs-2-november-2026)).
