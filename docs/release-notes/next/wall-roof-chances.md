**Chances for walls and roofs in the quick estimate.** Beside the glass's chances, each building
now gets three, drawn over the yield, the loads' scatter and an assumed strength scatter, and the
view can colour by them:
- that its walls are damaged (moderate or worse);
- that its walls are damaged heavily or worse, illustrative for concrete;
- that its roof is damaged (moderate or worse).

Against Beirut's runs at ten yields, each held out:
- **Concrete walls** (skill 0.55) are calibrated: each tenth of chance is within 0.1 of the runs.
- **Roofs** (skill 0.75) read 0.11–0.16 low in the middle.
- **Masonry** reads high.
- **Failure** gets no chance.

Headless, `--surrogate` writes them as `walls`
([glass by chance](../../glass-probability.md#walls-and-roofs)).
