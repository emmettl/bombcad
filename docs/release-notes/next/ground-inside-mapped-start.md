**Ground-shock points near the charge feel the blast's front again.** Since the mapped start became the default (18 October), the grid's clock begins when the one-dimensional solution's shock reaches its radius, up to 64 cells or 6 m/kg^(1/3) out, and a ground point inside that radius took its first load after the front had passed: under Xia et al.'s 1 t on 0.5 m cells, the soil column at 21 m read 0.068 m/s at 0.5 m deep instead of about 0.23. The frames before the clock now come from the one-dimensional solution itself, in the app, `BombCAD run` and `blastbench groundmotion`, and that column reads 0.249 m/s, within 5% of what Kingery–Bulmash's pulse gives it; points beyond the radius, the air and the run's digests are unchanged. Illustrative, as the ground-shock models are ([ground shock](../../ground-shock.md#before-the-mapped-starts-clock)).
