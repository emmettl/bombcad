**Charges fired one after another.** Each further charge now takes a delay after the main one: in
the sidebar's **More charges** (which also adds, places and removes further charges), with
`BombCAD run --charge <kg>@<x>,<y>,<z>+<ms>`, or as `delay` in a layout. The run lays each down
when its clock reaches it: with the fine start, from its own one-dimensional solution where the
air about it is still until that solution is due, otherwise as a ball of hot gas, and the run says
which and why. Gauges, peaks, impulses and the injury plane take each charge's records at its own
times. Against the same two charges run without interruption on cells four times finer, a charge
delayed 1, 5 or 20 ms reads as the undelayed one does: the delay adds no error of its own
([validation](../../validation.md#delayed-charges-19-october)); no open test of delayed charges
was found to compare with. See [a finely resolved
start](../../air-blast-model.md#a-finely-resolved-start) for what is approximated.
