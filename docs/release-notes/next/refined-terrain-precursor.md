**Refined air over terrain no longer stirs the still air beside buildings ahead of a blast.**
Where a refined patch's face met coarser air beside a building standing in cut ground, the finer
level gave the coarser cell the pressure on the whole face though that cell had counted only the
terrain's open share of it, and the difference pushed small cells beside the building every step:
hundreds of pascals to kilopascals in still air, most with two levels, and in Beirut's district an
early arrival about 0.7 kPa ahead of the shock at the south-east gauges. The push now goes through
the face's open share only; the air ahead of a blast stays within a pascal
([terrain](../../terrain.md)). Runs over terrain without buildings standing in it are unchanged.
