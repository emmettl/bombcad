**In the Quarry Face and ridge cut scenes, the unbroken rock's surface reads more like rock close in.** The face's joints are long, irregular traces that run through several beds, now and then jog or stop at one, and come in clusters and gaps, so the face no longer reads as coursed masonry. The fine laminations within the beds are wavy and broken and fade as they come under a pixel, so the shale beds and the ridge's bands no longer show hatch lines. The quarry floor close in is gravel and fines instead of light squares on a grid, and a talus apron is banked against the face's foot past the strip's ends, where the rock ended in teeth. On the ridge, soil and turf lie in the rock's hollows and on its gentler tops and shale slopes, with rock showing on the steps, and the rock round the cut blends into the flanks' grass at its edge.
