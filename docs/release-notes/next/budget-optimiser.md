**Best for a budget:** in the explorer's Protect the buildings, and in the workbench's What if, a
sheet asks for a budget and finds the barrier walls, the film or laminated glass building by building,
and the stand-off that leave the fewest people hurt (or the least glass thrown). It searches through
the learned response, in 0.3–2 s on the made-up square and street and 2–4 s on Beirut on a Mac Studio,
drawing its best walls in the view as it goes. The full estimate then judges its best few plans, as on
a let-go, and the best by it is put in the scene as a what-if. On the square at $1M, that is a 120 m
stand-off and two walls, a quarter fewer people hurt. In all 25 cases judged, the pick beat
hand-placed plans of the same budget. The costs are illustrative dollars from open price guides,
editable in the sheet ([protective design](../../protective-design.md#the-best-protection-for-a-budget-3-november-2026)).
