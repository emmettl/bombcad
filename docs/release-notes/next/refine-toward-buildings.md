**Refine toward the buildings that matter.** Click a building in the view and turn on **Refine
toward this building**, for as many as matter: the run sharpens the shock only along corridors from
each charge to them, and the rest of the scene stays on the coarse cells. On Beirut each chosen
building came out as refining everywhere gives it, where the coarse cells got two of three wrong,
for about 0.4 of the refined GPU time. The sidebar gives the advisor's estimate against refining
everywhere; saved runs keep the choice, and `scenebench district-compare --refine-toward` does the
same headless ([refinement steering](../../refinement-steering.md#refining-toward-chosen-buildings)).
