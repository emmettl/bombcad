**A large-panel block's collapse depends on the ground's friction as well as its element.** The
collapse's standing (still illustrative) now says what Coulomb friction on the ground (0.6, an
option, off by default) does to the whole-event runs: the debris comes to rest, 99% of it within
1.5–7.4 m of the plan where the frictionless runs ended at up to 16.9 m and still creeping, and the
three-storey block stands with today's element (none of its floor down, against 15%), its broken
ground-floor wall held at the foot. Assumed strain still brings down 74% of its floor, and twelve
storeys lose 237 m² against 245. No default changed. For developers, `blastbench wholeevent
--ground-friction 0.6`. See [ground friction](../../whole-event.md#ground-friction-30-october-2026).
