**A large-panel block's collapse is now quoted on a ground with friction, where its debris comes
to rest.** Whole-event runs take Coulomb friction 0.6 on the ground by default (the structure
solver's own default stays frictionless). The collapse's standing, still illustrative, quotes its
range between today's element and assumed strain on that ground. Twelve storeys lose 237–309 m² of
floor, 11–12 storeys past 15%, with 22,600–52,400 elements failed and 34–78 t of rubble. Three
storeys lose none to 74% of their floor. The debris comes to rest, 99% of it within 1.5–7.4 m of
the plan (5.8–7.4 m on twelve storeys, 1.5–5.2 m on three). The frictionless runs had ended at up
to 16.9 m and still creeping. On the frictionless ground, three storeys lost 15% of their floor with
today's element, where with friction their broken ground-floor wall holds at the foot and the block
stands. For developers, `blastbench wholeevent --ground-friction 0` gives the frictionless ground
of the earlier runs. See [adopted: ground friction 0.6](../../whole-event.md#adopted-ground-friction-06-30-october-2026).
