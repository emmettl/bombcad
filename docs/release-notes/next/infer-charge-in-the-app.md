**Infer the charge from arrivals, in the app.** Simulation ▸ Infer Charge from Arrivals… reads
observers' arrival times (a CSV, or Rigby et al.'s table) and works back to the charge's yield and
place with their 68% and 90% intervals, and a clock offset or the burst's height if asked, off the
main thread with progress. A plan draws the observers and the place's credible region; the standing
is the table's, illustrative once the optional broken-glass survey is used; and Place Charge at
Median puts the scene's charge there, given the scene corner's longitude and latitude
([using the app](../../using-the-app.md#inferring-the-charge-from-arrivals)).
