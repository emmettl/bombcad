**A ridge cut now breaks into fewer, larger pieces by default, and its window follows the firings at about real time.** The cut's rock is cut into about 31,000 blocks rather than 96,000, with the bedding and joints cutting half as much, so the median piece is 0.82 m rather than 0.57 m and the broken crest reads as slabs and blocks a metre or two across rather than a carpet of small ones. The physics a frame while the rock moves falls by about three and a half times (10 ms against 38 ms), so following the first firing the window plays at 0.97 to 0.99 of real time where it played at 0.4 to 0.5, and the whole 20 s event is simulated in about 5 s. The window's Blocks choice still offers the other counts; a Quarry Face is unchanged. Illustrative: the pieces are stylised, not a fragmentation prediction ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Piece size" under stage 3e, and stage 3f).
