**Charges on terrain start from the accurate one-dimensional solution.** A charge sitting on
shaped ground was always started as a rough sphere of hot gas, instead of the one-dimensional
solution mapped onto the grid that flat ground gets. Its loads then depended on how fine the cells
were: on Beirut's district the blast's impulse read 14–17% low on finely refined air. The ground
under the charge is now treated as the mirror it is, along its own slope. A charge on a gentle
slope reads as on flat ground, and Beirut's impulse is the same at every refinement within 2%.
Runs of charges on terrain made before this read a lower impulse; flat ground is unchanged.
