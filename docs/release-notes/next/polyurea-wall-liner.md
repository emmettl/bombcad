**Polyurea liners on masonry walls.** A building's unreinforced masonry walls can be lined with
spray-on polyurea in the construction editor. The liner holds the masonry once it cracks and
carries the load as a membrane until it tears. In one-way damage a lined wall stays (heavy)
where a bare one falls. It is judged against one lined and one bare blockwork wall in the
University of Ottawa's shock tube (Ciornei, 2012), so its standing is illustrative:
- past the wall's thickness, 1.3–1.9 times the measured deflection;
- the liner tears late;
- the masonry underneath does not arch, as the tests' did.

FRP, steel-stud liners and fabric catchers are not offered, for lack of open tests
([liners](../../building-envelopes.md#polyurea-liners-on-masonry)).
