**In the Ridge cut window and its clips, the crest now heaves up as one dome before it breaks, and its dust jets from the rock's cracks and pours off the breaking mass instead of lying on it like fog.** The whole crest over the cut starts to rise together, about 1.5 s before the first firing, and stands about 5 m higher by the first firing and 8 m at its highest, so from the opening view the skyline over the cut is plainly raised before the firings break it open in their order; before, a bulge ran along the cut with the firings and the crest had risen about half a metre at the first firing. Thin jets of dust rise from a few cracks in its top as it starts to move, more as it rises; each firing's column now starts from the rock's top where the heave has lifted it, not inside it at the old ground's height; and the broken rock sheds dust as it goes, so the cloud comes out of the mass and follows it down the flank. The rock settles as before (no block moving more than 0.3 m in the last second) and the notch is clear by about 7 s. With the ridge's new larger pieces the window still follows the first firing at about real time; its dust step costs the same and its draw about 1 ms more while the ridge fires. The Quarry Face is unchanged.
