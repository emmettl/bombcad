Charge dragging now moves the K24, K40 and minimum-distance rings immediately. With Safety
distances and injury on, the viewport also shows the scene's stand-off lines, amber when the
charge crosses onto their guarded side. The Hazards section reports the distance to each line.
Before a run, amber and dark-red injury rings show the existing bare-TNT surface-burst reference
for a person in the open. They exclude shielding, reflections, terrain and charge-height effects,
and are omitted for multiple charges. After a run, injury contours still use the simulated field.
