**In the Quarry Face and Ridge cut windows and their clips, the face's dust bank now has depth, the ridge's plume leans downwind as it rises, and the crest's close view comes out of the dust after the firings.** At the face, the firings' dust differs more from one to the next (some weak, each thrown out its own distance and climbing its own height, from a few clumps up the face), so the bank's front is lobes at different depths with shadowed recesses and gaps instead of a flat wall; its lower edge over the pile is a paler cream-grey instead of a brown fringe; and the thin streaks through it close in are gone. The bank is as strong and lasts as long as before. On the ridge, the plume's dust is carried on by the wind aloft, so its head runs ahead of its base; the haze after the firings is broad soft patches instead of swirled marbling; the near flank's low dust lies as a broken sheet more than as stems; and the dust left low over the cut settles once the firings are done, so a camera on the crest sees the plain clearly from about 8 s (it was fogged to 12 s). Drawing costs about the same (within a few tenths of a millisecond a frame; the crest's close view about 1 ms less). Illustrative, like the rest of the window: dust for the eye, not a model of real dust, a dust hazard or a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md) and [docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3r").
