**A large-panel block's collapse now carries its dust, as a passive cloud (developers).**
`blastbench wholeevent` makes fines from the elements' fracture and crushing work and from a share
of the rubble's impact losses. The air carries them in two classes, 10 and 100 µm, each settling at
its own speed. The fines are counted as they settle on the ground, under the pile or on the block,
or leave the domain. Each sample writes the cloud's airborne mass, its height and reach, and maps of
the mass over each column of air. On twelve storeys, 46 kg of fines are made by 7 s and 30 kg are
in the air at 3.3 s. The cloud fills the 31 m domain, so its reach is the domain's, and 19 kg settle
on the pile and the block. The standing is illustrative: the energy per kilogram of fines is rock's,
and every mass scales as its inverse. Whole-event runs carry the dust by default. `--no-dust` leaves
it out, and a run saved without it carries on without it. With the dust on, the collapse is the same
to the bit, and nothing else changes. See
[dust, stage 1](../../whole-event.md#dust-stage-1-1-november-2026) and
[adopted: the dust](../../whole-event.md#adopted-the-dust-1-november-2026).
