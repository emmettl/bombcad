**Profiled sheeting's buckled flanges can keep their folds** (an option, `ProfiledSheet.foldMemory`,
`--fold` in the benches). A flange that has buckled is pulled straight in tension at the strength
of its fold's plastic hinges, not at its yield. Nothing is fitted. ADBLAST's cladding then ends
nearer level after the blast: the mean miss of its final sets falls from 66 to 27 mm, the 1.5 mm
sheets ending level or lifted as tested, and Langdon's panels hardly change
([profiled sheeting](../../profiled-sheeting.md#what-is-left-scoped)).
