**The Quarry Face's rock past both ends of the strip, its gravel and its chips look like rock, and the window uses about 0.5 GB less memory.** The face beyond the broken strip is covered with rock down to its foot instead of showing a smooth wall; the gravel between the blocks is crushed stone of the beds' colours, lit on one side, rather than dark gaps or terrazzo; the small chips thrown off the blocks are stones of many shapes in the colour of the bed they broke from, not one small polyhedron; ironstone and weathered faces are brown rather than orange; and the blocks break in a wider spread of shapes and sizes, limestone into larger, blockier pieces. The window keeps its meshes only on the GPU, so its footprint after a firing is about 3.0 GB instead of 3.5 GB, and it draws a little faster (about 18 ms of work a frame playing, from 20.5). Illustrative, for the look only: the beds and their breakage are stylised, and nothing here is a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md)).
