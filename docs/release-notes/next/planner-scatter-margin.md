**The protection planner can leave a margin for the scatter between sides, so its plans meet their
target on a run, not only on average.** The option is `scenebench protect --margin flag:2.5`, or
`"margin": {"kind": "flag", "value": 2.5}` in the inputs, and it is off by default. A side whose
plan passes on its chance, but not with its load 2.5 standard deviations of the façade terms'
scatter up, is given the next dearer glazing with a smaller chance. The planner was judged on the
sparse made-up district's runs at 0.5–50 t, with two seeds. With the clear-top terms
(`--terms clear-top`) and the margin, its plans meet the target in all 16 cases, the worst side at
9.4%, for 1.01–1.34 times today's cost. Today's plans meet it in 7 of 16, the worst side at 43%.
The planner still buys the stand-off before film, and no wall. The margin was set holding masses
out, on one made-up district and one target, so its standing is illustrative. Adopting it as the
planner's default, with the clear-top terms, is proposed for the user's decision. Details are in
[protective design](../../protective-design.md#a-margin-for-the-scatter-between-sides-25-october-2026).
`--option-table` writes each side's chances under each option, for tracing.
