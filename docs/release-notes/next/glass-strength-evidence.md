**Glass strength: the evidence for a decision.** Every judged glass comparison was rerun with the
panes 1.2, 1.4 and 1.6 times E1300's strength: Monk and Clubley's panes, E1300's threshold,
Osnes's laminated panes, Beirut against GEER, the façade surrogate, and protective-design plans.
Today's strength is the only one on the safe side everywhere. Stronger defaults leave plans at the
edge of the damage up to 46% cheaper, but unsafe if the glass is weathered. The default is
unchanged ([glass strength](../../glass-strength.md)).
