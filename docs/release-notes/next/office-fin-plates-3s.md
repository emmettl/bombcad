**The office on fin plates collapses.** Run on to 3 s under the kerbside 1 t, 428 of its 480 fin
plates have fractured, 34–40 on every floor. Beams freed at both ends have dropped 7.5–8.5 m,
about two storeys, and the slabs sag between the columns. The perimeter leans out up to 1.5 m,
and the side façade peels away. The columns themselves stand: only 2 of 16,080 member elements
have failed. The run stops with the floors still coming down. The collapse is illustrative,
compared with nothing measured, and the fin plates' fracture is checked only against slow tests
([office](../../office-tower.md#on-fin-plates)).
