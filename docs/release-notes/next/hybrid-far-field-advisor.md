**New District's far field weighed against this Mac, and its glass given chances.**
- **The hand-over.** With the cells left to the advisor, Lay Out runs the whole district in the
  air when it fits on 4 m cells within an hour, and no far field is needed. Otherwise it hands
  over at 12 m/kg^(1/3) and brings that in by tenths, to half and no nearer than 50 m, until the
  near run does. The sheet says which, and why.
- **Glass chances.** After the run, the far buildings' sides get glass chances drawn over the far
  field's scatter and each building's glass strength, and the view can colour by them.
