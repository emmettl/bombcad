**A new validation case: a steel column's web breached close in** (for developers). `blastbench
dinu` meshes a HEB260 column as shells that tear, under 1.8 kg of TNT at 0.2 and 0.5 m from its web
(the CODEC bunker tests reported by FRAMEBLAST), and under the full-scale building's 2.2 and 2.5 kg
at 0.2 m. On 10 and 5 mm shells the web holds at 0.5 m and is breached at 0.2 m, as tested, but
the breach is 0.9 and 2.2 times the reported 0.3 m, and on 20 mm shells it holds. The full-flanged
column is holed by 2.2 kg where the building's only dished. Nothing changes in runs; details in
[a column web breached close in](../../validation.md#a-column-web-breached-close-in).
