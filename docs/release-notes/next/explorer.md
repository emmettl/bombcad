**The explorer, for someone new to blast:** a new, blank window now opens on the explorer (View ▸
Explore, ⌃⌘E; View ▸ Workbench, ⌃⌘W, for the full app, and the choice is remembered). Choose the
built-in square, a made-up town square of 398 buildings, and click to put a charge in it, or choose a
named place; its size comes in steps of 50 kg, 500 kg, 5 tonnes and 500 tonnes of TNT, with a
slider between. The blast wave plays by itself with captions, then a strip steps through the glass,
the walls, the people and the fire, each in plain words with a "How sure?" line taken from its
standing. **Try something** pins the picture and makes one change: twice the charge (how much further
the windows break, and why it is about a quarter, not twice), the charge behind the brick terrace,
or security film on every window (less glass thrown into the rooms), each building coloured better,
the same or worse. People are shown only as where they are hurt and how much a change helps, never
as counts. It rests on the live models already in the app (the quick estimate, the learned
response, the live wave, the what-ifs, the fire after the blast); their standing is unchanged, and
the full estimate follows each change ([the explorer](../../explorer.md)).
