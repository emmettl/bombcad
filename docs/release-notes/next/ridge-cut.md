**File ▸ New Ridge Cut (Experimental) cuts a notch through a ridge: its crest heaves and breaks, the rock is thrown up and slides down both flanks, dust rises, and a notch is left with broken rock in and around it.** It is the Quarry Face window's other scene, chosen with Scene at the top of its sidebar, and shares the rest: the bedded rock broken into about 100,000 rigid blocks, the GPU solver, the dust, playing as it is simulated, the ground (a built-in ridge, an area of a terrain file, or the open project's terrain, which starts with a cut across its highest ridge), Record Clip, and the firing order, ripple and count. You place the cut by dragging its line across the ridge on the map or in the 3-D view (Place cut), and set its width and depth. It is illustrative, for the look of it: the cut's shape and its firings are stylised, nothing comes from the blast solver, and it is not a blast-design aid or a road-cut or excavation design; it gives no fragmentation, reach or safe distances ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md)).
