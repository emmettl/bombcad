**The steel column web case, judged against its test paper** (for developers). The bunker tests'
own paper (Dinu et al., Procedia Engineering 210, 2017) measures the web's breach at almost 500
and 600 mm, not the 0.3 m that 0.7.4's notes compared with, and gives the column as 1.99 m on
pinned ends with the beams' joint 0.3 m above the charge; `blastbench dinu` now builds that. At
the measured 27% tearing strain the breach is 0.65–0.78 of the measured length on 10 mm shells and
1.15–1.38 on 5 mm shells, and 20 mm shells hold. A tearing strain of 23% on 10 mm shells and 15% on
20 mm reproduces it: a rule that scales with the element's size, proposed, not adopted. Nothing
changes in runs; details in
[against the test paper](../../validation.md#against-the-test-paper-30-october-2026).
