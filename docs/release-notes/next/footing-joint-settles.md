**A structure on footings now settles under its own weight before the blast**, where before it
never did and started the blast unloaded. Its footing joints chattered at rest, a few cm/s at
microns: the joint was worked out from the scene's positions, tens of metres rounded to microns,
and its stiffness turned that rounding into a fresh traction every step. It is now worked out from
displacements ([footings](../../structural-model.md#footings)), and the frame on a hillside
settles in 0.18 s. Every footing moves a little: FoRDy's shaken events by up to 2.4 mrad in peak
rotation, FoRCy's slow test by under 0.004 in its moment ratios, Alsubaei's walls on footings
226 → 228 mm and 356 → 362 mm at the top ([validation](../../validation.md#perimeter-walls-under-field-blasts)).
A body still moving at the 1 s limit whose displacement has stood still over its last 5 ms also
counts as settled now; bodies that settled before are unchanged.
