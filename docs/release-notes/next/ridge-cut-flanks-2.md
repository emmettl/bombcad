**A ridge cut's flanks look more like a real hillside: rock outcrops that break through the grass, scree in lobed fans, a grassy near slope, stones and bushes you can pick out, and rock round the cut that is whole until it breaks.** The rock ledges on the slopes were boxy slabs in rows, like a ruined wall; each run of them is now one weathered layer of its bed following the slope, its top following the bed's dip with turf on it, its front in two or three uneven tiers cut by narrow joints, sinking into the ground at its ends and in gaps, with soil and scree banked at its foot and blocks fallen below it. Scree lies in fans with lobed, wandering edges below some ledges and below the rock round the cut, and the near flank in the opening view is mostly grass. The loose stones are larger and chunkier, lie in clusters with boulders at the fans' toes and a few half-buried in the grass; the shrubs are bushes of several rounded lobes in olive, grey-green and brown. The render-only rock round the cut is one continuous weathered surface, like the cut's own unbroken rock, rather than a mosaic of cells with grass between them. A thin pale line across the far plain is gone (a gap at the edge of the distant ground). All of it is render-only and procedural, chosen by eye; nothing of it reaches the solver, and the Quarry Face scene is unchanged ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3d").
