**Close calls refined by the solver.** Once the quick estimate is in, the app can run the air on the
nearest buildings whose chances lie between 0.2 and 0.8 for the colouring shown. It runs them on a
scene cut round them, with finer air along the way to each, within a wall-clock budget you choose
and can cancel. Each building's run result replaces the estimate's as the blast passes it, outlined
in green in the view and marked *refined* in the list. On Beirut at 0.5 kt the first answer came in
13 s and eight in 22 s.
