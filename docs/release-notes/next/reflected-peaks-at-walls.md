**How far reflected peaks fall short at a wall, by cells per charge length.** A reflected peak read
beside a wall comes out low wherever the cells there are coarse against the charge: at 1 m/kg^(1/3)
it is 38–40% of Kingery–Bulmash's with 19–20 cells across W^(1/3), and 62–69% with 37–50, and at
4 m/kg^(1/3) 83% with 19 and full with 37 ([air-blast model](../../air-blast-model.md#reflected-peaks-at-a-wall)).
Most of it is the cell beside the wall reading the reflected shock smeared over it, not the
incident wave. Gauges can read the pressure at the wall instead (`gaugesReadWallPressure`,
`--wall-read`, off), which brings peaks to 82–96% close in on 0.02 m cells and 69–93% on 0.25 m
for 100 kg, but overshoots the close-in impulse by 19–43%, so it is for peaks alone.
