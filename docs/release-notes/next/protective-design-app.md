**Protective design in the app, and glazing a plan can write.** Simulation ▸ Protective Design…
takes a target (the worst GSA condition allowed and the chance accepted), the glazing changes
allowed with your costs (security film, toughened, laminated, or filling the window in), searches
off the main thread on the quick estimate's loads, and ranks the plans, cheapest meeting the target
first, with the sides passing narrowly. Choosing a plan colours each side by its residual chance;
Apply Plan writes the plan's glazing into the project as each changed side's own glazing mix.
Glazing mixes now carry a glass type, so a plan is an ordinary envelope construction, judged and
confirmed (`scenebench protect --confirm`) by the envelope damage assessment's own path; map
façades' annealed glass is judged as before. Plans stay illustrative until a run confirms them
([using the app](../../using-the-app.md#protective-design)).
