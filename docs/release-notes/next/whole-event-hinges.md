**A building's collapse is still carried by the elements' stabilisation, and the element that
would hand it to the concrete is too soft once cracked:** on the large-panel block most of the
hourglass control's 60–76% share of the elements' work is elements warping in modes the one-point
element cannot see, not the floors' hinges, and moving its cap changes nothing. The element that
bends in its cracked concrete (`assumedStrainCracking`, an option) makes the work the concrete's
and brings the twelve-storey bay down as far (7% further by 3.5 s), a second sooner, at five
times the cost, but sends struck beams 20% further than their tests. Collapse stays illustrative
([whole-event simulation](../../whole-event.md#7-hinges-two-elements-deep-25-october-2026)).
Developers: `blastbench wholeevent --breakdown <checkpoint>` sorts a saved run's work by part and
state, and `blastbench hinge-sections` bends the block's sections on the CPU reference.
