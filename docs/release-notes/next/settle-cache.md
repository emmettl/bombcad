**A structure settled under its own weight once is not settled again.** Running the same building
again, after changing the charge, the air or nothing at all, takes its settled state from the
last run instead of settling it afresh: the office tower starts its blast about 3 minutes sooner
(176 s to 7 s), the twelve-storey tower 34 s sooner, and the run is the same to the bit. A change
to the building, its materials or settings, or a new build of BombCAD settles afresh
([settling](../../structural-model.md#settling-under-its-own-weight)).
