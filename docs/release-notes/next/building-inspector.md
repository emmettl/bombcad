**Click a building to see its damage.** Once buildings have been judged (a run's envelope damage, a
quick estimate, or a district's far field), clicking one in the view outlines it. The sidebar's
**Selected building** then shows its damage, what it is built of, and its sides' glass chances,
and says which judgement it is: the run's, the quick estimate's, or the far field's estimate for a
building beyond the air. The view also zooms out far enough to see a district's far buildings
([using the app](../../using-the-app.md#the-view-and-the-sidebar)).
