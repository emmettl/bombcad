**"Arching between returns" for envelope masonry walls:** an option in the construction editor,
off by default. Unreinforced masonry walls held at both upright edges resist by arching across
their width once cracked, the abutments taken as not giving. With it, the terraced row's solid
one-brick fronts stand, cracked, from 16.9 m under 96 kg, as the detailed shells' do, where
without it the envelope graded them fallen to 26 m. The damage references' brick walls come
within 0.79–1.30 of the detailed rotations (0.82–2.54 without), and freestanding walls are
unchanged. Its standing is illustrative
([building envelopes](../../building-envelopes.md#arching-between-returns)).
