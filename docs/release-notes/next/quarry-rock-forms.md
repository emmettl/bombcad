**On a ridge cut the outcrops, crags and fallen blocks read more as rock masses, and the crest's rock steps no longer run as unbroken ruled lines.** The outcrops on the flanks come in clusters with bare slope between, in varied sizes (some two beds high, some short, some long, some tipped a little down the slope) rather than one evenly spaced kind; the crags bow out from the hill and end in it, so seen from afar they are blocky masses with a lit top and shaded sides rather than plates on edge; fallen blocks and stones are no thinner than about half their length, so none stands as a blade. On the crest each bed's step grows and dies out along it, jogs where a joint crosses and is grassed over in stretches (the two strongest steps still read as long lines from the default view). Drawing only: the blasted rock, its pieces and how they move are unchanged, the frame time is unchanged within the runs' spread, and the Quarry Face looks as before.
