**File ▸ New Quarry Face (Experimental) breaks a hillside's face with a stylised ripple of firings, and lets you play with it.** The rock behind a strip you drag along a face, on the built-in hillside or a terrain you open, is cut by bedding and joint sets into about 100,000 slabs and wedges. Each block is a rigid clump of spheres on the GPU, and the blocks fall, tumble and settle in a pile while dust rises from each firing and from the blocks as they strike. You can orbit, scrub the 20 s event, and fire it again with a different order, ripple or number of firings; a readout shows the frame time. It is illustrative, for the look of a face being blasted: the firing patterns are stylised, nothing comes from the blast solver, and it gives no loads, fragmentation, reach or safe distances, so it is not a blast-design aid ([docs/hillside-blast.md](../../hillside-blast.md)).
