**Three parameters calibrated against 21 comparisons at once (a study).** The afterburning time,
the concrete's tension and the glass's flaw constant are calibrated together, a Kennedy–O'Hagan
posterior with a discrepancy for each regime over emulators of 169 runs. Today's afterburning
time and tension lie well inside their posteriors. Today's glass strength lies at the
posterior's weak edge, its safe side. No default changes. `BombCAD ensemble --design` runs
validation cases at chosen parameter values
([calibration](../../calibration.md)).
