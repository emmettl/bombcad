**A ridge cut's flanks now have crags, and their rock outcrops, stones and bushes hold up close in.** A few small cliffs of several beds stand out of the steepest flanks, away from the cut and the opening view's line to the crest, with turf on their tops, jointed faces that step down at their ends, and blocks and scree fallen below them. The smaller outcrops, which read as stacked bales close in, are now jointed blocks of their bed whose tiers come and go along them: some blocks stand out and some are set back or have fallen away, a layer is missing over a block here and there, the partings between layers close and open, the faces lean back or are undercut, and their edges are broken rather than rounded. They are shaded and lit on their own, with dark water stains, lichen, dark crusts and turf, sky light that falls off under overhangs and in joints, and a softer shadow that does not speckle under the low sun. The larger stones are rounded or squared off as weathered stones and fallen blocks with smooth shading, rather than faceted like gems, and the bushes keep a lit, rounded crown far off, with a few bigger ones, so they read as shrubs in the wide view rather than dark dots. All of it is render-only and procedural, chosen by eye; nothing of it reaches the solver, and the Quarry Face scene is unchanged ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3g").
