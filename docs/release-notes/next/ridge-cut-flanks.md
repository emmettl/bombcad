**A ridge cut's flanks now read as ground: dry grass and low scrub on the gentler slopes, bedded rock ledges that stand out of the steeper ones, and scree below the ledges and below the rock round the cut.** Until now the ridge was paved with flat render-only slabs from its crest down to about the cut's floor and on its steepest ground, which from the new close opening view read as cut-outs with ground showing through. Dense rock is now only the cut's and a ragged margin round it, dipping under the ground at its edge; elsewhere the slopes carry ledges of the harder beds (each a slab of its bed standing out of the slope on its downhill side, its top the bed's top), loose stones on the scree, shrubs on the grass, and a ground whose gravel, grass tussocks and soil keep their detail close in. The far plain is patchy grassland with paler bare ground and darker scrub rather than one flat colour. All of it is render-only and procedural, chosen by eye; nothing of it reaches the solver, and the Quarry Face scene is unchanged ([docs/ridge-cut.md](../../ridge-cut.md), "Stage 3b").
