Fourth build of BombCAD, an interactive blast simulator for Macs.

- macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon (arm64), Metal GPU
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, with the ticket stapled: it opens without Gatekeeper warnings
- Version 0.2.0, build 6, from commit 5d930d7

What's new since 0.1.2:

- **Every result carries its evidential standing.** Peaks, impulses, deflections, damage and the effects beside the blast each say what they rest on (compared with measurement, verified against theory, an approximation, or illustrative), as a badge with its evidence, in the Run tab, in comparisons, in exports and in headless runs. Where the validation record covers the scene, results also carry **numeric error bands**: the range of model over measured for each gauge and for the structure, read from the record's tables, with which way the model errs and whether the error lies within the reference's own scatter. The bands come from comparisons the model's defaults were chosen with in view; none has yet been tested blind. An independent review of the validation record, and a protocol for blind tests (none scored yet), are in the docs.
- **Choosing settings.** An advisor finds the cheapest grid, refinement and afterburning whose error band meets a target, or the narrowest band within a time, by those same bands and a cost model checked against timed runs (Run tab, and `BombCAD run --advise`). It is only as good as the bands.
- **Scenes of your own.** Import OBJ, STL and IFC buildings (IFC as structures of shells and beams); edit structures, materials, openings and supports; save projects with their runs, compare runs, and sweep parameters, on several Macs if you have them. Scenes hold up to 16 structures, which meet through contact, and exposure-only building envelopes make neighbourhoods of dozens of buildings.
- **More of the air.** A second level of refinement near the shock; gravity, sub-grid mixing, an extinction limit for afterburning and radiative cooling as options. A heightfield **terrain**, from shapes or a DEM. **Large scenes**: charges up to 10 kt over kilometres, with cells scaled to the charge, a domain editor and lines of gauges, compared with Kingery–Bulmash to 40 m/kg^(1/3).
- **More of the structure.** Footings on layered soil, connections at any angle and embedment; freestanding boxes (checked against theory, not a blast test) and illustrative cars that slide, tip and collide in the blast; hourglass control that follows cracking and bars that slip, as options; and new comparisons, among them footings shaken at their base, slabs with steel in both faces, beams struck to a shear failure, and debris off slabs under contact charges.
- **Effects beside the blast**, one way and all illustrative: a cased charge's fragments; the fireball's thermal radiation, ray-marched on the GPU, and the surfaces it heats (work paused); the fireball's rise and cloud in a measured or standard atmosphere; ground shock; and a gas deflagration with vent panels as a second kind of source. The fragments, the radiation and the ground shock can each be reckoned on another Mac, placed by cost.
- **Out of the app.** `BombCAD run` runs a project headless, and runs export to USD with OpenVDB volumes for rendering.

Known gaps, as the standing says. Several err on the unsafe side, predicting too little damage:

- Close-in charges do too little damage: slabs a third to a half as far down as measured, too little spall, never holed by default.
- Shear is too strong: one beam without stirrups 11–15% strong on fine meshes and 37% on coarse ones. Short beams struck to a shear failure fail by the wrong mechanism, and those with stirrups hold where the tests' struts crushed.
- The internal-explosion chamber's roof is too stiff: left a sixth to a quarter as far up as measured.
- Debris is held back rather than thrown; collapse has never been compared.
- The fireball's radiation is too bright and vented gas peaks too low; both are illustrative.
- The slab test reads high under its own load and low under a lower one.
- Peak pressures read low unless the air is refined.
- Structural results depend on the mesh and are not shown to converge.

`docs/validation.md` and `docs/validation-review.md` give the details.

Download `BombCAD-0.2.0-macos-arm64.zip`, expand it and move `BombCAD.app` to Applications. To check the download:

```
shasum -a 256 -c BombCAD-0.2.0-macos-arm64.zip.sha256
```

It is a study of the numerics, the physics and the performance of blast simulation, not a design tool, and nothing in it should be used to judge the safety of a real structure. MIT licence.
