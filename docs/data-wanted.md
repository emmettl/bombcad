# Data wanted

What would test the model and is not yet in hand, as things to do: documents to fetch in a
browser, datasets to ask their owners for, and, last, what has been fetched and where it is
used. The full survey behind this list, each source with what it would give and what was
searched, is in [Data survey](data-survey.md).

## To fetch

Open documents that this machine's scripts cannot download: the sites refuse automated access.
In a browser each is a click. Save each into the folder given, with a `SOURCE.md` beside it (the
citation, the link and the date), and tell a session it is there; nothing goes into the
repository. DTIC's links are the public PDFs as its listings gave them on 11 October 2026; if one
fails, search the number at [discover.dtic.mil](https://discover.dtic.mil/).

A cloud session tried every source below on 18 October 2026 and fetched none of them. Its
container's network policy refused every host but GitHub and the package registries (DTIC, OSTI,

what it found are routes, not documents: corrected titles, direct links, open mirrors and prices,

full in the session's report, kept outside the repository at

The [calibration](calibration.md#the-answers) of 20 October ranks two of these documents by how
much they would narrow the model's parameters, each by pinning a regime's own error:
- **Beauchamp and Matalucci's annealed panes.** Panes that held beside panes that broke would
  narrow the glass's flaw constant most: its posterior about 68% narrower. **Obtained 21 October 2026, and
  not that:** eleven of its twelve panes broke, most of them weakened on purpose, and the one that
  held was 1/2 in thick, so it gives no threshold. Its filmed speeds are compared instead
  ([validation](validation.md#sandias-windows)).
- **Kingery's BRL 1344 (below).** Its data by distance, with their scatter, would narrow the
  afterburning time about 48%.

The calibration's [step 2](calibration.md#step-2-fracture-energy-and-slabs-that-read-the-air-and-the-concrete-22-october-2026)
(22 October) reranks them. Each figure is for a regime's error known to within ±5%, and in
brackets known exactly:
- **The air:** the incident impulse's scatter by distance (BRL 1344, or Snowball's and Dial
  Pack's gauge reports) narrows the afterburning time 31% (45%).
- **The glass:** E1300 is out of the likelihood, as a model, not a test.
  - The break times' own error pinned, which Monk's thesis trials with their repeats might do,
    narrows the flaw constant 19% (23%).
  - Johns and Clubley's threshold panes would be a new regime, which the linearised ranking cannot
    score: they remain the glass to fetch first.
- **The concrete:** wanted are repeated tests, not new kinds:
  - repeats of drop-weight beams such as Saatci's and Peterson's narrow the fracture energy 29%;
  - repeats of Wu's slab narrow the tension 18%.
