# Reynolds's normal-strength 8 in. slabs, RSCR4-6: inputs for an open comparison

These are the companions in K. A. Reynolds's MS thesis (UMKC, 2015) of the record's calibrated
contest slab: the normal-strength series, RSCR, with No. 3 Grade 60 bars at 8 in. in the same
blast load simulator. The judge transcribed them after [blind test 3](../../../docs/validation.md#blind-test-3-reynoldss-high-strength-slabs-in-the-blast-load-simulator)'s
reveal. This is an **open** comparison: the measurements are in `inputs.json` with the inputs,
and the result is calibration, not a blind test.

What the thesis gives, all in `inputs.json`:

| Item | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Average peak pressure and impulse, RSCR4-6 | Table 15, p. 71 | No pressure history: the thesis plots and tabulates only the HSCV slabs' |
| Peak and residual deflection, RSCR4-6 | Table 15, p. 71 | No time of peak, no history |
| Concrete cylinders, 7, 14 and 28 days | pp. 14-15 | 34.7-41.4 MPa; age at test not stated |
| Bars | p. 15 | "Conventional Grade 60"; no yield or ultimate given for these slabs |
| Layout | Fig 4, p. 10 | As the HSCV 8 in. slabs (`../inputs/slabs.json`) |
| Damage | Chapter 8, p. 73 | Words only, no photographs |
| The lower shot, RSCR3 | Table 15, p. 71 | 80.5 mm, against the record's 72 mm plateau, for reference |

The thesis questions nothing about RSCR4-6. The judge notes that RSCR4's residual is 95% of its
peak, where RSCR5's and RSCR6's are 44% and 61%. The thesis is silent on it, and gives no history
to check it against. The RSCR series was first published by Thiagarajan and Johnson (ACI
Structural Journal 111(6), 2014), which is not in hand and may hold the histories.
